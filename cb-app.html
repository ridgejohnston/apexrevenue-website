Getting Started

SETUP & INSTALL

Deploy the app through the Chaturbate Developer Portal. It takes about 5 minutes and requires no coding.

1
Open the CB Developer Portal
Go to devportal.cb.dev and log in with your Chaturbate account. Click Create New App or open your existing Apex Revenue app.
2
Paste the Shared Code
In the sidebar click Shared Code. Paste the contents of shared-code-additions.js first, then append transform-shared-code.js directly below it. These provide all helper functions the handlers depend on.
3
Load the Settings
Click Settings → Advanced Settings Editor (JSON view). Clear the existing content and paste the full contents of settings-merged-minified.json. This loads all 8 setting fieldsets with defaults.
4
Paste Each Handler
For each event in the sidebar, paste the matching handler file. Replace the entire default content — each file is a full replacement.
App StartApp StopBroadcast Start Broadcast StopCallbackUser Enter Fanclub JoinUser FollowUser Leave Chat MessageMessage TransformPanel Update Tip Received
5
Deploy to Testbed & Publish
Click "Update on testbed" to preview in a test room. Once everything looks right, click "Submit for review" to publish the app to the Chaturbate App Store. Existing installs auto-update.
6
Install on Your Room & Go Live
Find Apex Revenue in the Chaturbate App Store and install it on your broadcaster account. The app starts automatically when you begin a broadcast — type /apex help in chat to confirm it's running.
What It Does

FEATURES

Everything the app handles automatically once it's running in your broadcast.

Real-time Tip Tracking
Every tip is tracked per-session and all-time. Tracks session total, tip count, highest tip, unique tippers, and per-user lifetime totals for tier ranking.
Live Broadcast Panel
The panel updates automatically showing session tips, top tipper, and goal progress as a visual bar directly on your Chaturbate broadcast page.
Themed Announcements
Tip announcements, big-tip banners, goal progress, follows, and fanclub joins — all styled in your chosen color theme with customisable message templates.
Viewer Welcome & Onboarding
New viewers are welcomed automatically. Known tippers get a personalised return banner showing their lifetime tips and tier. Mods receive a private help menu on first enter.
Tip Goals with Progress
Set a token goal and the app announces progress at configurable milestones (e.g. every 25%). A visual progress bar fills in the panel. Goal-reached triggers a celebration banner.
Recurring Banners
Schedule a custom banner to post automatically every N minutes — great for tip menu reminders, social links, or promo messages. Fully configurable interval and message.
Follow & Fanclub Events
Automatic banners on new follows and fanclub joins. Viewer milestone banners fire when total viewers hit configurable thresholds (e.g. every 50 visitors).
Extension Overlay Sync
Every tip event emits data to your Apex Revenue browser extension in real time — feeding the whale tracker, AI tip prompts, and live earnings dashboard.
Chat Moderation (v1.2)
Keyword blocking, spam detection, caps limiting, link filtering, and automatic data masking (emails, phone numbers). Flagged messages whispered to broadcaster.
Message Styling & Badges (v1.2)
Role-based tier badges, background highlights for tippers, big tippers, fanclub, mods, and broadcaster. Every chat message styled to match their status.
Session Summary
When your broadcast ends the app posts a private session summary to you — total tokens, tip count, top tipper, and unique tippers for the session.
Auto-Announce Timer
Periodically posts an extension promotion announcement to your chat on a configurable interval — encouraging viewers to download the Apex Revenue extension.

v1.2 Feature

MODERATION & TRANSFORMS

The Chat Message Transform handler runs on every message before it's displayed — silently cleaning, filtering, and styling in real time.

Keyword Blocking
Set a comma-separated block list. Matching messages are replaced with ***, hidden, or flagged — your choice.
Spam Detection
Auto-detects excessive caps (configurable % threshold) and repeated characters (like "heyyyy"). Fixes or hides the message.
Link Filter
Blocks URLs and domains from non-privileged users. Tippers and fanclub members can be exempted with a minimum tip threshold.
Data Masking
Automatically redacts email addresses, phone numbers, and SSN patterns from chat — protecting your viewers and staying compliant.
Moderation logging
When a message is blocked, modified, or flagged the app can whisper the original message to you privately — so you always know what was caught and why. Toggle this in settings: Whisper moderation actions to broadcaster.
