Broadcaster Commands

FULL CONTROL
MID-STREAM

Adjust tiers, tokens, and goals in real time without restarting the app — all from the Chaturbate chat.

!
settier <n> <min> <max>
Adjust a vibration tier's token range live. The auto-cascade system automatically adjusts adjacent tiers to prevent overlap — no gaps or conflicts.
!settier 3 75 149 → sets Intense tier to 75–149 tokens
!
settok <amount>
Override the tip goal token target mid-session. Useful for special events or when you want to extend a goal round.
!settok 5000 → sets goal to 5,000 tokens
!
setreached <amount>
Manually set the current tokens-reached value. Use this to correct the displayed progress or sync after a network interruption.
!setreached 1200 → marks 1,200 tokens as received
SESSION & SYNC COMMANDS v0.2.0
/
sync
Announce Digital Sync to the room. Viewers with Bluetooth toys can visit the sync page and feel every vibration in real time through Intiface Central.
/sync → broadcasts sync URL and setup instructions to everyone
/
endcontrol
Immediately terminate the active Give Control session. Returns full toy control to the broadcaster. Owner/Mod only.
/endcontrol → ends current fan's control session instantly
/
extendcontrol <seconds>
Extend the current Give Control session by the specified number of seconds. Owner/Mod only.
/extendcontrol 30 → adds 30 more seconds to the active session

AUTO-CASCADE ON TIER ADJUSTMENT
When you use !settier to adjust a range, ApexSensations automatically expands or contracts adjacent tier ranges to maintain clean boundaries — no overlaps, no dead zones.

Setup Guide

BLUETOOTH
PAIRING GUIDE

ApexSensations controls your toy through either the Lovense Cam Extension or Buttplug.io. Choose the path that matches your device.

PATH A
Lovense Device
Lush, Nora, Max 2, Hush, Domi, Ferri, Diamo, Edge, Osci, Tenera
PATH B
All Other Devices
Kiiroo, We-Vibe, Satisfyer, OhMiBod, Magic Motion, and 200+ more via Buttplug.io
PATH A
Lovense Cam Extension
01
DOWNLOAD LOVENSE CAM EXTENSION
Install the Lovense Cam Extension for Chrome from the Chrome Web Store. This is a free tool provided by Lovense — it acts as the bridge between ApexSensations and your toy.
Get Lovense Cam Extension →
02
POWER ON YOUR TOY
Turn on your Lovense device. Most models power on by holding the main button for 2–3 seconds until the LED flashes. The light pattern indicates the device is in pairing mode.
03
CONNECT VIA THE EXTENSION
Click the Lovense Cam Extension icon in your Chrome toolbar. Select your device from the list and click Connect. The LED on your toy will stop flashing when paired successfully.
04
START APEXSENSATIONS IN YOUR ROOM
Go to your Chaturbate broadcaster dashboard, launch ApexSensations from the Apps tab, and start your stream. ApexSensations will automatically route tip commands to the Lovense extension — every tip triggers your toy instantly.
PATH B
Buttplug.io / Intiface
01
DOWNLOAD INTIFACE CENTRAL
Install Intiface Central — the free desktop app from Buttplug.io that connects your device to the web. Available for Windows, macOS, and Linux.
Download Intiface Central →
02
ENABLE BLUETOOTH ON YOUR PC
Make sure Bluetooth is turned on in your PC's settings. On Windows: Settings → Bluetooth & devices. On macOS: System Settings → Bluetooth. Your toy must be within 10 feet of your computer.
03
POWER ON YOUR DEVICE AND SCAN
Turn on your toy and open Intiface Central. Click Start Server, then click Scan for Devices. Your device will appear in the list — click it to connect. A green indicator confirms it's paired.
04
KEEP INTIFACE RUNNING IN BACKGROUND
Leave Intiface Central running while you stream. ApexSensations communicates with it in the background — every tip command routes through Intiface to your toy automatically. Do not close Intiface while live.
05
START APEXSENSATIONS IN YOUR ROOM
Launch ApexSensations from your Chaturbate Apps tab and go live. Your tips will now control your device through the Intiface connection — test with a small vibe command before starting your public stream.
⚡ QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING
Toy not responding to tips
Ensure the Lovense extension or Intiface is running and your device shows as connected. Refresh your Chaturbate tab and restart ApexSensations.
Device not showing in scan
Power off your toy, wait 5 seconds, power back on, and scan again. Make sure it's within 10 feet of your PC and no other app has claimed the Bluetooth connection.
Connection drops mid-stream
Bluetooth range and interference are common causes. Keep your device within 6 feet of your PC and away from other wireless devices. A USB Bluetooth dongle can improve reliability.
Lovense app conflicts
Close the Lovense mobile app or desktop app if running — only one application can hold the Bluetooth connection at a time. Use only the Cam Extension while streaming.
